Privacy-first design. DutyMate is designed to process roster information primarily on the user's device. AAW Technologies does not sell roster information to advertisers.

1. Overview

DutyMate is a duty-roster viewing, history, calendar and reporting application developed by AAW Technologies. The app supports Personal Mode and Professional Mode.

The current app does not require an AAW Technologies account or a separate AAW Technologies roster server for normal roster viewing. Some optional features use Google services, including Google Sign-In, app-specific Google Drive backup, Google AdMob advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

2. Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, DutyMate may process:

  • Your name, designation, organization name and selected employee profile.
  • Shift codes, duty dates, leave entries, roster changes and calendar notes.
  • Calculated duty hours, day/night duty totals, leave history and report summaries.
  • In Professional Mode, employee names, designations, teams/groups, shift assignments, leave entries and related roster information contained in the roster source you connect.
  • App preferences, shift mappings, colors, source settings and refresh metadata.
  • If you use Google Sign-In, basic account information such as your Google email address and display name.

DutyMate does not receive your Google password. Google authentication is handled by Google.

3. Roster sources and imported files

DutyMate can read supported roster information from shared Google Sheets or Drive sources, and from Excel or PDF files that you explicitly select. The app reads the roster content needed to identify employees, dates, shift codes, groups, designations and related details.

Imported files are selected through Android's file/document access system. DutyMate does not require unrestricted access to every personal file on your device simply to import a roster. A connected source link or selected document reference may be stored locally so the app can reopen or refresh the same source later.

Professional Mode may display information about other employees. You should use Professional Mode only when you are authorized to access and process the relevant roster information.

4. Information stored on your device

DutyMate stores app settings and roster-related cache locally so that the app can work efficiently, preserve historical information and support offline viewing. Local data may include your selected employee, roster snapshots, historical roster data, Full Roster data, shift mappings and colors, calendar notes, report data, Personal/Professional mode preference, source settings and backup settings.

Local app data normally remains on the device until it is replaced, cleared through Android app settings, or removed when the app's data is deleted or the app is uninstalled, subject to Android's behavior.

5. Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup

Google Sign-In is optional. It is used for DutyMate backup/restore features and may also be used for entitlement-related functions where applicable.

If you enable Google backup and sync, DutyMate uses Google Drive's private app-specific appDataFolder storage rather than requesting general access to all files in your normal Google Drive. The hidden DutyMate backup may contain saved roster history, selected employee information, organization details, shift mappings, notes, report-related data and relevant settings required to restore DutyMate on another device.

Signing out stops DutyMate from using the signed-in Google session. A backup already stored in your Google account may remain there until it is replaced or removed using the applicable Google/account controls.

6. PDF, Excel, save, print and share

When you create a PDF or Excel report, DutyMate generates it from roster information available in the app. Personal reports may contain your own roster information. Professional reports may contain multiple employees' names, designations, shifts, leave dates and calculated duty hours.

Reports are saved, printed or shared only when you choose the relevant action. After a report leaves DutyMate, its handling is controlled by the destination app, storage provider, printer, recipient or service that you select.

7. Advertising, identifiers and consent

Eligible Free Personal Mode may display Google AdMob advertising. Depending on the screen and app state, this can include adaptive banner ads, interstitial ads at natural navigation breaks, and App Open ads during eligible app starts. Premium and Professional experiences are intended to follow their configured ad-free access rules.

Google Mobile Ads may process information used to deliver, limit, measure and protect advertising. Depending on your region, consent choices and Google configuration, this may include device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app/device information, ad interactions, diagnostic information, approximate location derived from signals such as IP address, and consent-related information.

Before eligible ad requests, DutyMate uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to refresh the applicable advertising-consent status. If Google determines that a consent message is required, a Google-managed privacy message may be displayed. Where an ongoing privacy-options entry point is available, DutyMate provides an Ad privacy choices entry in Settings so users can review or update applicable choices.

AAW Technologies does not sell DutyMate roster information to advertisers and does not use roster contents to create advertising profiles.

8. Premium features and payments

DutyMate may offer Personal Premium, Professional Premium and Complete Premium access levels. When production in-app purchases or subscriptions are enabled, payments are processed through the applicable app-store billing system, such as Google Play Billing.

AAW Technologies does not need your full payment-card details to provide app-store purchases. Google Play or another applicable store may process purchase, payment, account and transaction information under its own terms and privacy policy.

10. How information is used

DutyMate uses information to:

  • Display your roster, calendar, duty history and reports.
  • Calculate duty hours, shift totals, leave information and roster summaries.
  • Load and refresh a roster source you connect.
  • Provide Professional Mode roster viewing and reporting when authorized.
  • Save your settings, notes, history and app preferences.
  • Provide optional Google account backup and restore.
  • Generate files that you choose to save, print or share.
  • Display advertising to eligible Free Personal Mode users in accordance with applicable consent choices.
  • Maintain app security, reliability and expected functionality.

11. Sharing and sale of information

AAW Technologies does not sell roster information or employee roster data to advertisers. Information may be processed by third-party service providers only when you use a feature that depends on that provider, such as Google Sign-In, Google Drive backup, Google AdMob/UMP or Google Play.

When you intentionally share or export a roster/report, the information is sent to the destination you choose. You are responsible for having appropriate authorization before exporting or sharing information relating to other employees.

12. Data retention and deletion

Local roster history and settings may remain for as long as you keep DutyMate app data because preserving history is part of the app's functionality. You can remove local DutyMate data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app, subject to Android behavior.

Local deletion does not necessarily delete a previous Google Drive app-specific backup. Cloud backup data is associated with the connected Google account and is subject to the backup and account controls made available by Google and DutyMate.

13. Your choices and controls

  • You can use core roster features without signing in to an AAW Technologies account.
  • You choose whether to connect Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup.
  • You can sign out of the Google account used by DutyMate.
  • You select which local files or supported roster sources DutyMate can read.
  • You decide when to create, save, print or share a report.
  • Where applicable, you can review or change Google advertising consent choices through the privacy message or DutyMate's Ad privacy choices setting.
  • You may use Android system settings to manage app permissions, notifications and local app data.

14. Security

DutyMate uses platform-provided authentication, scoped file access and app-specific Google Drive storage where applicable. Reasonable efforts are made to limit access to what a feature needs. However, no app, device, network or cloud service can guarantee absolute security.

You should protect your device, Google account, exported reports and any roster files containing personal or organizational information.

15. Children's privacy

DutyMate is designed for duty-roster use in work and organizational contexts and is not specifically directed to children. Professional Mode should not be used to process children's personal information unless the user has a lawful and appropriate reason and authorization to do so.

16. International processing

Third-party providers such as Google may process information on servers located in different countries. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms, privacy policies and applicable legal safeguards.

17. Changes to this policy

AAW Technologies may update this Privacy Policy when DutyMate features, data practices, advertising configuration, billing, legal requirements or third-party services change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised when a new version takes effect.
