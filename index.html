FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need an account to use DutyMate?

Core personal roster features are designed to work without a separate AAW Technologies account. Some optional connected features, such as backup or Team Workspace, may require Google Sign-In.

Can DutyMate show my whole team roster?

Yes. Full Roster and Professional Mode are designed for authorized team roster viewing, while Team Workspace adds managed shared-roster collaboration.

Can I import an existing roster?

DutyMate supports approved roster formats including supported Excel and PDF imports, plus configured roster sources and templates.

Can I export reports?

Supported reports can be exported to formats such as PDF and Excel, with save, print or share options depending on the feature and access level.

Does DutyMate contain ads?

Eligible Free Personal Mode may contain advertising. Applicable premium or professional access can provide an ad-free experience where configured.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

The current DutyMate Privacy Policy is available at dutymate.aawtechnologies.com/privacy.
